About

Cash flow,
without the runaround.

Zap Funds is an Australian invoice finance broker. We exist for one reason: too many good businesses sit on unpaid invoices while the bank takes weeks to answer the phone.

We do one thing: invoice finance. Unlocking the money already owed to you — up to 90% of an invoice's value within 24 hours — through a panel of 200+ lenders. One enquiry from you, written options from us, and you compare real numbers side by side before you sign anything.

We are a broker, not a lender. Lenders pay our fee, so it costs you nothing upfront. And if invoice finance is not the right fit, we will tell you straight and point you to whoever can help.

What we stand for

Written, lender-confirmed numbers. No ballpark guesses, no call centres, no starting over. You deal with one person who knows your file and answers the phone.
