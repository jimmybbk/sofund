Industries

Invoice finance for
IT and professional services

IT contractors, consultancies and professional services firms bill on 30 to 60 day cycles, and the payroll for your project team runs weekly or fortnightly. Winning a bigger engagement means hiring before the first invoice pays, which is exactly backwards for cash flow. An invoice finance facility aligns the money with the work.

Why cash flow is hard in professional services

  • Monthly billing cycles against fortnightly payroll.
  • Enterprise clients take 45 to 60 days and pay exactly to terms.
  • Growth means hiring contractors before the revenue arrives.
  • Milestone billing gets delayed by sign offs and procurement.

How a facility covers the gap

An invoice finance facility advances up to 90% of your service invoice within 24 hours of it being issued. Contractor payroll, software costs and overheads are covered while the client pays on their terms. Take bigger engagements without hiring on credit cards.

A worked example

Your consultancy invoices a client $90,000 for the month for a project team on 45 day terms. Contractor payroll is due before that invoice pays.

ItemAmount
Invoice value$90,000
Advance at 85%$76,500 within 24 hours
Balance until the client pays$13,500
Client pays on day 45$90,000
Balance released to you, minus fees$13,500

$76,500 this week, so the team is paid and the engagement keeps growing.

Who qualifies

Monthly service invoices to business clients, signed statements of work or timesheets, and creditworthy customers. Solo consultants through to firms with full delivery teams.
