Invoice finance for
IT and professional services

The IT contract, professional, and consultancies market bills on 30 and 60-day terms, whilst payroll for staff is weekly or bi-weekly. There needs to be contractors and consultants in place long before their time can be charged for, meaning that winning a big gig creates exactly the sort of cash flow issue which led you down this path to begin with! An invoice finance facility places money squarely at the most crucial pinch points.

Why cash flow is hard in professional services

  • Monthly billing cycles against fortnightly payroll.
  • Enterprise clients take 45 to 60 days and pay exactly to terms.
  • Growth means hiring contractors before the revenue arrives.
  • Milestone billing gets delayed by sign offs and procurement.

How a facility covers the gap

An invoice finance facility that advances up to 90% of your service invoice within 24 hours of being issued. Covering contractor payroll, software costs, and overheads, the client then pays on their terms. With such a fast money-pipeline, you can finally accept that massive engagement without hiring on credit cards.

A worked example

Your consultancy charges $90,000 for the month to a 45-day term client for your project team. Contractors are paid in advance of the invoice.

ItemAmount
Invoice value$90,000
Advance at 85%$76,500 within 24 hours
Balance until the client pays$13,500
Client pays on day 45$90,000
Balance released to you, minus fees$13,500

The team gets paid and the engagement keeps growing, $76,500 this week.

Who qualifies

Creditworthy customers Invoices and sign off (monthly invoices to business customers for completed assignments. Sometimes accompanied by sign sheets or statements of work) firms with a staff complement running full-scale delivery through to small-time solo agents

Common questions

Do consultancies use invoice finance?

Wide. Companies operating a contractor model are not reliant on receiving a payment from the customer to issue payroll.

What documents are needed?

A signed Statement of Work or Timesheets, the invoice, and the customer agreement.

Can I fund one client invoice at a time?

Yes, selective facilities fund individual invoices, which suits project-based consulting work.
