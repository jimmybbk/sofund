Industries we support

Any business that invoices customers on payment terms uses invoice finance. No industry feels the pressure of having a gap between carrying out работы and getting paid differently; therefore, we have produced a specific guide to some of the sectors we are most active in.

Choose your industry

  • Transport and freight: fuel, tolls, repairs and driver wages go out daily while freight invoices pay at 45 days.
  • Recruitment and staffing: weekly payroll for placed workers against monthly or quarterly client terms.
  • Manufacturing: materials and production runs paid upfront, large orders paid 60 to 90 days later.
  • Wholesale and distribution: stock purchased upfront and retailers paying at their own pace.
  • IT and professional services: consultancies hiring project teams long before the first invoice pays.
  • Cleaning and facilities: wages every week or fortnight against monthly commercial contracts.
  • Engineering and trade services: milestone claims, queried invoices and retention money on commercial projects.
