Invoice finance for
engineering and trade services

Engineering and trade services work on commercial projects with milestone claims, retention money and long subcontracting chains. Materials are bought upfront, wages run between milestones, and progress claims get queried before they get paid. An invoice finance facility funds the gap between the claim and the payment.

Why cash flow is hard in engineering

  • Progress claims can be queried or delayed by head contractors.
  • Materials and fabrication are paid upfront for work delivered months later.
  • Retention of around 5% is held until practical completion or beyond.
  • Subcontractors on your projects expect payment between your milestones.

How a facility funds the milestones

An invoice finance facility advances up to 90% of a certified progress claim or completed project invoice within 24 hours of submission. Materials, fabrication and wages between milestones are covered. Properly served claims under Security of Payment legislation are especially straightforward for providers to fund.

A worked example

Your firm issues a $120,000 milestone claim on a commercial project with 60 day payment terms. Fabrication costs and wages are due now.

$102,000 this week, keeping the project and the workforce moving.

Who qualifies

Served progress claims or completed project invoices to commercial customers, undisputed work, and proof of delivery or certification. Engineering firms, fabricators and trade contractors from single crews to multi project operations.

Common questions

Can I fund progress claims?

Yes, particularly claims that are certified, admitted or supported by an adjudication outcome under Security of Payment legislation.

What about retention money?

Retention is generally not fundable until it is presently payable, since it is not yet due. Specialist options exist; ask before signing.

Are disputed variations eligible?

Usually not. Disputed variations and uncertified claims are harder to fund until they are agreed.
