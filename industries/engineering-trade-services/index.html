Invoice finance for
engineering and trade services

Typical commercial projects like trade services and engineering ones involve milestone payment claims, retention money and long subcontracting chains; the materials are purchased upfront, wages are run till the next milestone claim, and progress claims get queried before they can be released against payments due. An invoice finance facility funds the gap between claim submission and getting paid for payments due from invoice factoring companies.

Why cash flow is hard in engineering

  • Progress claims can be queried or delayed by head contractors.
  • Materials and fabrication are paid upfront for work delivered months later.
  • Retention of around 5% is held until practical completion or beyond.
  • Subcontractors on your projects expect payment between your milestones.

How a facility funds the milestones

A construction/invoice finance company can front you up to 90 per cent of the value, with cash in your bank account, of a certified progress claim (even better if claims are served to Security of Payment legislation requirements) or completed project invoice within 24 hours of submission. Wages bill, materials, and subcontracts are underwritten between milestone projects.

Who qualifies

Have submitted progress claims or completion invoices with undisputed work and POD or certification to commercial clients, typically engineering firms, fabricators, and trade contractors, ranging from single crews to multi-project operations.

Common questions

Can I fund progress claims?

Yes, as specified with reference to payment claims that have been either certified, admitted, or awarded under Security of Payment legislation.

What about retention money?

In most cases money retained is not capable of being funded by a factoring company until it is currently payable because it is not due to be paid. There are niche products for this so you should ask if applicable before signing.
