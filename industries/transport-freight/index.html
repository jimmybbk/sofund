Invoice finance for
transport and freight

When it comes to transport and freight, the costs never wait for the freight invoice. Card fuels, tolls, repairs, insurance, wages all hit on weekly or monthly while your customers are typically on 30, 45, even 60 days. The numbers work only when the money arrives faster than the bills, and invoice finance is well-equipped to achieve that.

Why cash flow is hard in transport

  • Fuel and tolls are charged daily or weekly, no matter when the customer pays.
  • Drivers and owner-operators expect payment per run or per week.
  • Maintenance, insurance and registration cannot pause while an invoice ages.
  • One large slow paying customer can lock up six figures of working capital.

How a facility keeps your fleet moving

An invoice finance facility that advances you up to 90% of a freight invoice within 24 hours of it being raised. You can be assured that fuel, tolls, and wages are paid on time. You can quote for bigger contracts with the knowledge that funding scales with your ledger. Should you want your customers not to know a finance company is involved through a confidential arrangement, there is that option as well.

Invoice National Retailer for distribution run of 45,000 on 60-day terms this week. The fuel and wages associated with the run need paying this week.

Who qualifies

Freight forwarders, or even customers or retailers. Undisputed & valid invoices. The owner-drivers and even larger fleets can invoice business on terms, and the facility can work. Freight Forwarders (customers), Retailers (customers), Manufacturers (customers), Fuel Companies, etc. Proof of Delivery, e.g., signed PODs.

Common questions

How fast can a transport invoice be funded?

With funding now in place, eligible invoices with proof of delivery from both sides, if requested and attached, are funded normally within one working day of submission. If approved, eligible freight invoices with proof of delivery attached will normally be funded within one working day of submission.

What do I need to qualify?

Proof of delivery along with other invoices from authenticated business-to-business customers, including retailers, manufacturers, and freight forwarders. It must not be consumers' petrol card invoices.

Will my customers know?

Not with invoice financing in principle (invoice factoring only). As with invoice discounting, you own customers continue to pay you.
