Invoice finance for
wholesale and distribution

Not only that, at the heart of their business models, distributors and wholesale services operate at two degrees removed. They have cash tied up in the stock, waiting for retailers to finally clear the invoice (in their own sweet time - obviously), thus every new sales opportunity in retail implies a rise in the exposure, while the money necessary to go back on the quest for stock remains in the book records.

Why cash flow is hard in wholesale

  • Stock is purchased upfront, before demand is certain.
  • Retailers pay at 45 to 90 days, and the big chains pay slowest.
  • Range expansion and seasonal stock absorb working capital.
  • Restocking delays mean empty shelves and lost orders.

How a facility turns the ledger into restocking cash

Up to 90% of a retailer invoice will be advanced within 24 hours of issuing the invoice. This cash then stocks the shelves immediately, allowing bigger retail accounts knowing the funding grows as the ledger grows and seasonal stock pushes do not drain the business.

A worked example

A supermarket chain places a distribution order with your firm for £80,000 on 45-day credit. You have received another order, and the stock needs purchasing now before receiving money from the supermarket.

ItemAmount
Invoice value$80,000
Advance at 85%$68,000 within 24 hours
Balance until the customer pays$12,000
Customer pays on day 45$80,000
Balance released to you, minus fees$12,000

Stocked the next order without waiting since 68000 was released.

Who qualifies

Uninsured invoices to business customers like supermarket chains, retail & commercial buyers with undisputed receipt of goods. From local warehouse type of distributor going onto sites.

Common questions

Can I fund invoices to supermarket chains?

Although their credit terms are long, large chains get an edge over smaller customers due to credit strength.

Is stock also financed?

All invoices for standard facilities are payable, not for stock. Some providers provide this, so do ask when considering a quote or signing up to a facility.

What documents do I need?

This must include a copy of the customer agreement together with a valid tax invoice and proof of delivery.
