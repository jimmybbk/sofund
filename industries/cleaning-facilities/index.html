Industries

Invoice finance for
cleaning and facilities

Commercial cleaning and facilities businesses run on weekly and fortnightly wages against monthly contract invoices, often across multiple sites with thin margins. The work is delivered every night, but the money arrives once a month, and a new site win means hiring before the first invoice clears. An invoice finance facility aligns the payroll with the contract payments.

Why cash flow is hard in cleaning and facilities

  • Payroll is the biggest cost and runs weekly or fortnightly.
  • Corporate clients pay monthly or quarterly, sometimes late.
  • Winning a new site means extra staff, equipment and consumables upfront.
  • Thin margins leave little room for a slow month.

How a facility covers the contracts

An invoice finance facility advances up to 90% of your monthly contract invoices within 24 hours of issue. Payroll is covered on time across every site. Take on new sites and bigger contracts knowing the cash flow scales with the work, not the payment terms.

A worked example

Your company invoices $40,000 for the month across several commercial sites on 30 day terms. Wages for those sites are due before the client pays.

$34,000 this week, covering wages across every site.

Who qualifies

Monthly commercial contract invoices, verified site delivery, creditworthy business clients. Solo operators through to multi site facilities companies.
