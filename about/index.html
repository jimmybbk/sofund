About

Cash flow,
without the runaround.

Zap Funds is an Australian invoice finance broker. We exist for one reason: too many good businesses sit on unpaid invoices while the bank takes weeks to answer the phone.

We do one thing: invoice finance. Unlocking the money already owed to you, up to 90% of an invoice's value within 24 hours, through a panel of 200+ lenders. One enquiry from you, written options from us, and you compare real numbers side by side before you sign anything.

We are a broker, not a lender. Our fee comes from lenders, not you. If invoice finance is not right for you, we will tell you straight and point you towards someone closer to a solution to suit your business.

Who we help

Any Australian business that invoices other businesses on 30, 60 or 90 day terms. We work with transport and freight operators, recruitment agencies, manufacturers, wholesalers, cleaning and facilities companies, IT contractors and consultancies and engineering firms, alongside builders and subcontractors across the construction industry.

What we offer

Invoice factoring, confidential invoice discounting and selective invoice finance. Whole ledger or single invoice facilities. Advances up to 90% of invoice value, settled when your customer pays, with the funding limit growing as your sales grow. Read the details in our guides to invoice factoring, debtor finance, business cash flow and working capital.
