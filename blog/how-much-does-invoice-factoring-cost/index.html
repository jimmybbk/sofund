How much does invoice factoring cost?

Zap Funds | 9 August 2026 | 6 min read

The truthful response is "it depends". Factoring fees are not just one single rate; instead, they are based upon your sales, your clients, and the time taken for an outstanding invoice to be paid. With invoice factoring, we can give you details of each individual charge that adds up to your total amount, the things that encourage higher charges, and how you should be distinguishing various providers to locate the right one for your organisation.

The three fees that make up the cost

1. The discount or funding fee

This is the biggest cost element and is calculated on the amount you draw and the length of time your invoice remains outstanding. In general, companies will charge you either a rate per 30-day period with more charges the longer your customer takes, or a rate that reduces as your customer's creditworthiness and payment periods fall.

2. The administration fee

It includes operation of facility by process invoices, ledgers & making collections. If there is such arrangement with the Factor, it could also be either in the form of flat monthly charge or as per the amount of cash drawn on a daily basis.

3. The establishment fee

Setting up fee: This is a once-off payment at the set-up stage and covers the onboarding, the checks, and documentation.

What drives your rate up or down

  • Customer quality: invoices to large, creditworthy businesses with a record of paying are cheaper to fund than invoices to unknown customers.
  • Time outstanding: invoices paid in 30 days cost less to fund than invoices that run to 90.
  • Volume: higher and consistent invoicing earns better rates.
  • Industry: some industries carry higher risk and price accordingly.
  • Disputed invoices: contested claims are harder to fund and can attract higher fees.

A worked example

Assume they factor $80,000 of invoices each month at a funding cost of 2% per 30-day period plus charges a $500 monthly administration fee:

ItemAmount
Invoices factored$80,000
Advance releasedUp to 90% of face value
Funding fee at 2%$1,600
Administration fee$500
Total monthly cost$2,100

This is illustrative only. All providers price differently; therefore, compare the quotes that you receive.

How to compare providers properly

Each provider quotes differently - compare the combined total of fees for the same loan requirement, not a figure in isolation. Ask each of them for:

  • The funding fee rate, and whether it steps up the longer an invoice runs
  • All administration and establishment charges
  • What happens to fees if your customer pays late or disputes the invoice
  • Minimum usage or contract terms, and the notice to exit

Then compare the total monthly cost of the two at your invoicing volumes, not the headline total cost.

Is factoring worth the cost?

In reality, it comes down to a comparison between the real costs (lost early payment discounts, overdraft interest, etc.) of waiting for payments versus the cost of factoring. For a company with strong customers and long terms, factoring is often cheaper than what you lose waiting. If you have thin margins and dubious customers, this would never stack up, and an honest broker would tell you.

Frequently asked questions about factoring costs

How much does invoice factoring cost?

Costs They are usually charged as a discount fee on the amount funded plus establishment and administration costs, typically per 30-day period. It depends on your volumes, customers, and how long invoices stay outstanding.

Why does factoring cost more than a bank loan?

The credit risk of the invoices is undertaken by the factor. The collections are also factored by the factor and advances in terms of days instead of weeks (if not more). What a factor can offer to the company is prompt processing and thus a credit risk and credit management free factoring service is extended to you for a sum of money.

Do fees increase if my customer pays late?

Usually, yes. The longer the time an invoice is outstanding, the longer funding comes off it, so if an invoice is 30 days old, a lower funding fee can be used, whereas 90 days would be higher.

How do I get the lowest rate?

Check your written quotes from all vendors and keep your invoices clean and undisputed and ensure your customers are creditworthy. A rate comparison by broker clearly shows every rate side by side.
