Instant invoice finance

Zap Funds | 30 September 2026 | 6 min read

It can be tempting to click on 'instant funding' adverts when trying to meet the demand of your employees but awaiting payment of your invoices for up to two months. Below is an honest guide to invoice finance; what kind of funding you can expect to receive instantly, what will take a few days, and how you can do everything in your potential to speed along your funding, allowing for a fast process as possible.

What instant invoice finance actually means

There are two stages, and they move at different speeds.

Stage one is the facility approval. The finance provider assesses your business, your invoices and your customers before the first dollar moves. This is the slower part, and any provider promising instant approval without assessment is not telling you the whole story.

Stage two is the ongoing funding. Once the facility is live, submitting an eligible invoice is genuinely fast: the advance lands in your account within 24 hours, and with some providers the same day. Every invoice after the first is quick because the assessment has already been done.

The instant invoice finance model will require just one short set-up, then finance will be provided against new invoices at speed and as frequently as you like.

How the fast funding works

With an approved facility, the invoice cycle is short in each case:

  • You complete work for a business customer and issue an invoice.
  • You submit the invoice through the provider portal, with proof of delivery attached.
  • The provider verifies the invoice against the approved facility.
  • Up to 90% of the invoice value lands in your account, commonly within 24 hours.
  • Your customer pays the invoice, and the remaining balance is released to you minus fees.

What makes funding genuinely fast

The funders that provide money fastest tend to have similar requirements. However, since there are so many of these funders, it should be possible to find a match.

  • Invoices to business customers, not consumers.
  • Undisputed debts for work that is verifiably completed.
  • Creditworthy customers with a record of paying their invoices.
  • Clean documentation: the invoice, the contract or purchase order, and delivery evidence.
  • Digital submission through a portal rather than paper forms.

What is not on the list: property security, years of financial statements, or a perfect trading history. It is the invoice and the customer that is assessed.

What slows funding down

  • Disputed invoices, or customers with a history of contesting claims.
  • Missing documentation: no proof of delivery, no signed agreement.
  • First time applications with no invoicing records to verify.
  • Consumer debtors. Invoice finance funds business to business invoices only.

Selective facilities for one off gaps

No, you do not necessarily need a permanent faculty to single source your finance needs. With selective invoice finance, you can do this for just the single invoice or a small group of invoices, enabling one-off contract work and to bridge some of the gaps you might have over a seasonal period. Given there is less of the organisation involved and the assessment is limited to invoices, it is often the quickest facility (sometimes known as an invoice discount facility or a debt finance facility) to arrange.

Does instant funding cost more?

Speed is not the premium. It is priced on the amount funded, how long the invoice stays outstanding, and your customer's credit risk. Do compare the total costs on the same scenarios between the providers and read what happens if your customer pays late.

Who uses instant invoice finance

The companies making the most use of it are those for whom slow terms present costs on a daily basis: transport operators covering fuel and wages, construction contractors paying sub-trades between progress claims, recruitment agencies meeting payroll on a weekly basis, wholesalers restocking for retailers who pay slow so they can get by another week.

Common questions

Is same day funding really possible? Yes, for eligible invoices inside an approved facility. Submissions verified in the morning are commonly funded the same or next business day.

What slows the first approval down? Missing documents, disputed invoices, consumer debtors, or customers with poor payment histories. Clean paperwork and verified customers are what speed approval up.

Can I fund just one invoice? Yes. Selective facilities fund individual invoices, which suits one off large contracts or occasional cash flow gaps.

Does instant funding cost more? Speed itself is rarely the premium. Fees are based on the amount, the time the invoice stays outstanding and the customer credit risk.
