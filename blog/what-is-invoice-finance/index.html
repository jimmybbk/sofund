What is invoice finance?

By Wayne Clothier · 21 November 2026 · 4 min read · Zap Funds

Invoice finance is a business funding solution that lets Australian businesses access money tied up in unpaid customer invoices. Instead of waiting 30, 60 or 90 days for payment, businesses can get an advance against eligible invoices to help manage cash flow.

How does invoice finance work?

  1. Your business supplies goods or services.
  2. You issue an invoice to your customer.
  3. A finance provider advances an agreed percentage of the invoice.
  4. Your customer pays the invoice.
  5. The remaining balance is settled after fees and charges.

That is the whole cycle. Once a facility is in place, most businesses repeat it with every new invoice, so funding grows naturally alongside sales.

Who uses invoice finance?

Any business that invoices other businesses on payment terms can use it. The industries we see most often include:

  • Construction businesses
  • Transport companies
  • Manufacturers
  • Wholesale businesses
  • Recruitment agencies

What they have in common is simple: real work done, real invoices issued, and customers who pay on terms. If that sounds like your business, the money tied up in your debtor ledger is funding you are not using yet.
