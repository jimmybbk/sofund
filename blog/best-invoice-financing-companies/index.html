Best invoice financing companies

Zap Funds | 25 September 2026 | 6 min read

If you were to gather ten Invoice Finance Providers in a room and question them on who the most superior in the market is, you will be met with ten differing answers. They are not all wrong. A certain Invoice Finance company would be best placed for the working of civil contractors but could not possibly fulfill the same requirements a recruitment company would hope to be dealt; this may vary completely in turn when it comes to the work required of a wholesaler. What is the best in our field is a partnership. We have found the best for our business and a strong sense of a professional work ethic would stand by the need for each individual business or company to choose accordingly.

Therefore, instead of rating or classifying the current invoice financing providers/companies around, this guide has chosen to share information about the checklist top invoice factoring companies share, the differences between great providers and pricey ones, as well as several considerations from clients.

What the best invoice financing companies have in common

It does not matter what size or type of provider, the good ones agree on these points:

  • They advance between 80% and 90% of the invoice value, and they say so upfront.
  • They fund approved invoices within 24 hours of submission.
  • They put their fees, recourse terms and notice periods in writing without being chased.
  • They assess your invoices and your customers, not your house.
  • They have experience in your industry and understand progress claims, timesheets and retention.
  • They will decline a deal that does not fit rather than sign you into something expensive.

The types of companies offering invoice finance

Banks. The major banks offer invoice finance to established businesses with strong financials and clean ledgers. Pricing can be sharp, but the assessment is slower, the paperwork heavier, and the security requirements stricter. Banks suit businesses with years of financials and property or strong asset backing.

Specialist non bank lenders. These providers focus on invoices and customer quality rather than property, move in days rather than weeks, and understand industries like construction, transport and staffing. They are the most common fit for small and mid sized businesses.

Fintech platforms. Digital onboarding, online portals and fast selective funding for single invoices. Suits businesses that want speed and flexibility for occasional gaps.

The red flags to avoid

  • Fees that are quoted as a range but never confirmed in writing.
  • Minimum term contracts of 12 or 24 months with exit penalties.
  • Minimum usage clauses that charge you for invoices you did not fund.
  • Vague answers on what happens if a customer pays late or disputes an invoice.
  • Guaranteed approval marketing. No legitimate provider guarantees approval before assessment.

How to compare providers properly

Compare quotes for the exact same situations, including presenting the information in the same format. It is a good thing to ask every provider you engage with for the total bill once your customer pays at 30 days, once after paying at 60 days, and, in a similar fashion, at 90 days. Get clarification of funding percentage written down and ask whether it is recourse and non-recourse and what notice period you must give to leave - also, whether customers are notified? Then you just compare answers. The moment quotes are laid out similarly, it is a lot easier to compare.

Common questions

Who are the best invoice financing companies in Australia? The best provider for your business depends on your industry, your invoice volumes and your customers. Specialist non bank lenders suit fast moving industries, banks suit established businesses with strong financials, and a side by side comparison shows you which is which.

What advance rate should I expect? Most providers advance between 80% and 90% of an eligible invoice value. Rates below that range need a clear justification in the fee structure.

How long does approval take? Specialist lenders can approve new facilities in a few days. Banks typically take weeks because of full credit assessment processes.

Are banks better than specialist lenders for invoice finance? Neither is automatically better. Banks suit established businesses with strong financials; specialist lenders move faster and assess invoices and customers rather than property.

How much does invoice finance cost? The main cost is the discount or funding fee on the amount advanced, plus administration and establishment fees. Compare the total cost against the benefit of having the cash weeks earlier.
