How we actually work

Real quotes.
Not ballpark guesses.

Most brokers shop your file around and come back with indications — numbers no lender has committed to. We work the other way: we only show you figures a lender has confirmed in writing for your situation.

It means the rates you see from us are the only ones you can sign. We will notify you of all changes in interest rates before they are actually settled and offer you the alternative options, if any.

The fee we charge is paid by lenders, not you. If we will not be able to get a better deal than you have, we will point you to someone better and tell you to go away.

Invoice finance,
explained plainly.

Use the outstanding invoices that you have against customers as collateral for loans. This type of finance allows immediate access to cash without waiting for 30, 60 or even 90 days. Get 90% of your outstanding invoices to a specialist provider of Invoice Finance within 24 hours of accepting the proposal, who will provide the balance once the customers have paid the due amounts to the providers.

Facilities come in different shapes: invoice factoring where the provider manages your collections, confidential invoice discounting where your customers keep paying you directly, and selective facilities that fund single invoices. Whichever structure fits, the funding follows your sales, so it grows as you grow.

Explore by industry: transport and freight, recruitment and staffing, manufacturing, wholesale and distribution, IT and professional services, cleaning and facilities and engineering and trade services.
