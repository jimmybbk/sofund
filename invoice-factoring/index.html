Invoice factoring

Invoice factoring,
made simple.

Invoice factoring is a form of invoice finance where a business sells or assigns its unpaid invoices to a finance provider. The provider advances most of the invoice value upfront, then collects payment directly from your customers.

How invoice factoring works

1. You complete work for a business customer and issue an invoice.

2. The finance provider advances you up to 85% of the invoice value, often within 24 hours.

3. Your customer pays the finance provider directly, per the arrangement.

4. You receive the remaining balance, minus the provider fees.

Who invoice factoring suits

  • Businesses that want to outsource invoice collections
  • Operators without a dedicated accounts team
  • Owners who would rather spend time on work than on chasing payments
  • Companies needing working capital without property security

Factoring vs invoice discounting

Factoring is disclosed: your customers know payments go to the provider, and collections are handled for you. Discounting is confidential: your customers keep paying you directly and your accounts team manages the ledger. The right choice depends on how you want your customers handled and how your administration is set up.
